## Third-party software

My Paint has no third-party dependencies. It is written in Swift and uses only the frameworks that ship with macOS (AppKit and its companions).

## Artwork and text

All icons, textures, images and interface texts in My Paint are original: they were created for this project (the images are drawn in code) and are covered by the Apache License 2.0. The application icon (a standing blue-framed canvas with “My”, a rainbow stroke and a brush, in the style of the My Notepad icon) is generated by `Tools/icon.swift` into `Resources/MyPaint.icns`.

No artwork, icon or text was copied from Microsoft Paint or Microsoft Windows.

## Fonts

My Paint does not include any font files. The interface uses the system font of macOS. The Selawik font is not included; whether to bundle it, under its own license, is still an open item.
